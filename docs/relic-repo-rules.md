## THE SHORT, SHORT VERSION

### ATTEMPTS, CHECKS, AND EFFORT VS. ROOM TARGET
* Stop looking up individual enemy Armor Classes. The GM posts a single Room Target (e.g., 12).
* Roll a d20 + Stat on an Attempt to meet or beat it. Checks are the same; if something is being done to you, d20 + Stat to meet or beat it and avoid the bad stuff.
* If you succeed on an Attempt, roll Effort to see how much work you actually accomplished: Basic (d4 for bare fists/wits), Weapon/Tool (d6), Magic (d8 for spells), or Ultimate (d12 bonus when you roll a natural 20).
* If an Attempt is Hard, the Target goes up by 3. If it's Easy, the Target is lowered by 3.

### HEARTS AND EFFORT
* Everything, including you, the monsters, the gear you're carrying, and the door you're trying to open have Hearts representing how much effort it will take to overcome it. 1 Heart = 10 Effort/HP.

### DEATH, DYING & STAT DECAY
* When you drop to 0 HP, immediately reduce one relevant base Stat (at the GM's choosing) by 1.
* If all relevant stats hit 0, congratulations: you've officially run out of body parts to sacrifice and you're dead.

### THE KARMA SYSTEM
* Everyone starts each session with 3 Karma.
* Roll a natural 1? Your misery increments your Karma by +1 (up to a max of 6).
* Need to land a crucial hit? Spend Karma to add +1 per point to any roll. Have more than 1 Karma and are really desperate? Burn all your Karma at once to force any roll to become an automatic 20.

### SPELLCASTING AS PHYSICAL INVENTORY CLUTTER
* **Wizards:** Must physically equip each spellbook in an inventory slot.
* **Sorcerers:** Store spells in their blood, taking up carried slots because hosting raw magic causes physical heartburn.
* **Bards:** Must physically play an equipped instrument to cast Spellsongs.
* **Clerics & Paladins:** Clerics carry a single Deity's Prayer Book for all their prayers, while Paladins carry a Holy Libram to smite things into absolute oblivion.
* **Everyone Else:** Spells are one use items. Once it's cast, the item magically (POOF!) disappears.

### GEAR AND WEALTH
* Inventory is strictly capped at 10 Equipped and 10 Carried slots.
* You have a Wealth Stat (0 to +6). Want an Uncommon item? Make a Wealth Check. Failed? Use the "Safety Valve" and permanently reduce your Wealth stat by 1 to force the merchant to take your money.
* Acquiring gear from a market may require a Wealth check. Common? No check. Uncommon, Wealth Check against the Market's Target. Rare, Hard Wealth Check.

## CHARACTER CONVERSION

For each character you want to convert from HiSD&D to ICRPG:

1. Take the Race and Class from your HiSD&D character and note it as your Bio-Form and Profession, respectively, on your ICRPG Character Sheet.
2. Allocate six, +1 bonuses among the Stats (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA), Armor, and Effort (Basic, Weapons/Tools, Magic, Ultimate) 'Base' values. Add in any bonuses from your Bio-Form to the Base value as well. You may use the suggestions from your Profession for Stat allocation, but you are not required to.
3. Add the Starter Gear from your Profession under 'Loot' on your ICRPG Character Sheet and mark it as 'Equipped'.
4. Pick one Milestone item and add it to your Loot, Equipping it if necessary.
5. Pick up to 3 weapons from the Weapons list on p.19. Add these to your 'Loot' and 'Equip' at least one of them.
6. Set your Wealth to 1 and your Karma to 3.
7. If your Starter Gear involves Spells, Companions, or other items of note, add them under Loot or Powers and Abilities.
8. You will start with 1 Heart of HP (10hp) and 0 Mastery Points.

## RACES/BIO-FORMS

> *"Look, I don't care if your bloodline traces back to a cosmic golden dragon or an ancient elven deity. To the Great Ledger, you're just a 10-slot inventory rack wrapped in meat, and all of you owe back-taxes."*

### HUMAN
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 INT, +1 Weapon Effort
* **Adaptable Heroism:** Humans add a +1 bonus to all Checks and Attempts when spending Karma points to boost a roll.

### DRAGONBORN
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 STR, +1 CON
* **Elemental Breath:** Choose a type (acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison). Once per Room, as an Action you can exhale in a Close cone. All targets in the area must make a DEX or CON Check vs. the Room Target or take Magic Effort.

### DWARF
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 CON, +1 STR
* **Darkvision:** You can see clearly in pitch-black darkness.

### ELF/HALF-ELF
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 DEX, +1 INT
* **Fey Grace:** Once per Round, when you fail a DEX Check or Attempt, you may reroll it and use the better result.

### GNOME
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 INT, +1 WIS
* **Arcane Sense:** You automatically detect the presence of active magic within Near range. You know the general direction of the magic, but not its exact location, nature, or school.

### HALFLING
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 WIS, +1 Magic Effort
* **Halfling's Luck:** Whenever you roll a natural 1 on any D20 Attempt or Check, you may immediately reroll the die (keeping the second result, while still gaining +1 Karma as normal).

### HALF-ORC
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 STR, +1 CON
* **Savage Might:** If you roll max damage on Weapon Effort, roll again and add the new roll to your total.

### TIEFLING
* **Stat Bonuses:** +1 CHA, +1 INT
* **Hellfire Legacy:** You can cast Burning Hands once per Room via a CON Attempt vs. the Room Target without requiring an inventory slot.

## CLASSES/PROFESSIONS

> *"Your Profession tells us who you are. Your gear tells us what you can do. The story tells us what you become."*

### MONK
Unarmored, swift-striking masters of physical and spiritual discipline. While other warriors rely on heavy plates of steel and massive broadswords, the Monk transforms their own body into a state-regulated weapon of kinetic foreclosure, using focused inner energy to bypass physical security and execute perfect physical strikes.

* **ROLE:** Superhuman Agility, Unarmed Striking, and Rapid Infiltration
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 DEX:** Crucial for dodging dangerous traps, leaping over hazardous terrain, and coordinating high-speed martial strikes.
  * **+1 CON:** Essential for enduring frontline feedback when unarmored and surviving the wear-and-tear of physical combat.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Wrist-Wraps of the Tiger:** Any Close attack that misses you, you may immediately counterattack; make an Attempt and on a success do Basic Effort.
  * **Robes of the Zen Master:** While wearing no armor (Equipment providing + Armor), add your DEX to your Armor.
  * **Sandals of the Shadow:** You may move Far instead of the normal Close movement.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Tiger Claw Katars:** Steel claws that attach directly to your wraps. Unarmed attacks do Weapon/Tool Effort.
  * **Sash of the Thousand Fists:** Once per turn, you can attempt two unarmed attacks on your turn instead of one. The second attack attempt is treated as Hard.
  * **Ring of the Boundless Spirit:** A jade ring that acts as a spiritual anchor. Once per day, when your Hit Points drop to 0 or below, you may roll a WIS check against the Room Target. If successful, you regain 1 Heart of health and do not suffer the -1 Stat reduction.
  * **Incense of the Iron Palm:** Once per day, you may use an Action to light this special temple incense to channel your vital energy. Your Armor becomes 20 for 1d4 rounds.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Quivering Palm:** Once per Room, when you land a successful unarmed strike, you can channel your spiritual energy directly into the target's pressure points. The target must make a CON check against the Room Target. If they fail, your attack deals Ultimate Effort twice and they are Stunned on their next turn. If they succeed, they take standard Effort.

### WIZARD
The classic academic scholar of the arcane. Wizards do not rely on raw divine grace or volatile bloodlines; they treat spellcasting as a precise geometric science. Every cosmic law must be mapped, researched, and recorded in a physical spellbook that they must physically carry and equip to wield.

* **ROLE:** Arcane Versatility, Spellbook Management, and Area Manipulation
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 INT:** Vital for deciphering ancient ruins, copying complex magical formulas, and successfully casting INT-based spells.
  * **+1 Magic Effort:** More bang for your buck.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Spellbook (2):** Holds one spell from the Wizard Spell List. Spellbooks must be equipped in your inventory to be cast.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Grimoire of Yog:** An ancient spellbook reinforced with crystal fragments. This masterwork book is so finely organized that it holds up to 3 equipped Wizard spells within its single inventory slot (saving you 2 Equipped slots). Spells cast from it have Magic Effort dice that explode on a 7 or an 8.
  * **Sage's Hourglass:** Worn on a belt chain. Once per session, when you fail a Spellcasting Attempt, you can flip this hourglass to reverse time and immediately reroll the Attempt.
  * **Ring of the Preserved Mind:** Once per day, when your Hit Points drop to 0 or below, the ring flashes to protect your mind. You regain 1 Heart of health, and the -1 Stat reduction is applied to a physical Stat (STR, DEX, or CON) of your choice instead of your casting stat (INT).
  * **Amulet of Arcane Siphoning:** Whenever you roll a maximum result on a Magic Effort die when damaging an enemy, you siphon the residual energy. You immediately heal 2 HP or make an ally's next Attempt Easy.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Spell Mastery:** Choose one Spell in your repertoire. You no longer need the Spellbook for it in your inventory as it is fully committed to your memory. In addition, the Spell is amplified; pick one: it does ULTIMATE Effort, Attempts are Easy/Checks are Hard, or its duration is increased.

### BARD
The ultimate artistic mimic of the Glimmer-Gloom. Bards bypass standard class restrictions by physically writing and composing their own magical Spellsongs during Downtime Actions, adapting spells from any school of magic into evocative, named melodies that they channel through their equipped musical instruments.

* **ROLE:** Songwriting, Adaptive Support, and Creative Deception
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 CHA:** Essential for writing your custom Spellsongs and performing captivating melodies that bend reality.
  * **+1 DEX:** Important for rapid, flawless fingerwork on stringed instruments and swift physical evasion.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Classic Lute:** The essential acoustic conduit required to focus and cast your Spellsongs. You must actively play your equipped musical instrument to cast.
  * **Spellsong:** You start with 1 Spellsong of your choice (derived from any class list) already written and equipped in an inventory slot.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Lute of the Lost Epoch:** A legendary masterwork instrument inlaid with cosmic mother-of-pearl. Spellsongs cast using this lute deal +1 Magic Effort.
  * **Cloak of the Jester-King:** A patchwork mantle of defensive silk. Once per Room, when an enemy within Near range attempts to attack you or an ally, you can flourish your cloak and play a jarring, mocking chord. The enemy must make a WIS Check against the Room Target. If they fail, their attack is disrupted, and they are forced to target a different, random creature of your choice.
  * **The Maestro's Baton:** Worn tucked into your sash. Once per Room, when an ally within Far range fails a roll, you can flick this baton as a Simple Action. The ally may immediately re-roll their failed Check or Attempt as an Easy roll.
  * **Leather Songwriter's Folio:** A sturdy, brass-clasped binder of raw parchment and charcoal. When writing a new Spellsong during a Downtime Action, your CHA casting Attempt is always Easy.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Grand Finale:** Once per Room, you can perform a breathtaking musical crescendo that lasts for 1d4 rounds. While maintaining this Grand Finale, all allies within Far range gain a +1 bonus to all Attempts and Checks, and your successful Spellsongs deal Ultimate Effort (d12) instead of standard Magic Effort (d8). You cannot take an Action, Simple Action, or Reaction while you maintain the Grand Finale.

### RANGER
Apex scouts of the wild, blending pinpoint ranged accuracy with a deep, telepathic connection to a loyal beast companion. Rangers channel Druidic magic strictly through natural Rune-Stones, though their practical nature restricts them to casting these spells as slow, out of combat Rituals.

* **ROLE:** Pinpoint Marksmanship, Scouting, and Coordinated Companion Tactics
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 DEX:** Vital for high-speed ranged accuracy with heavy warbows and quick evasion through dense brush.
  * **+1 WIS:** Essential for tracking elusive targets, spotting traps, and successfully casting primal Druidic Rituals.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Heartseeker:** All Attempts with one weapon (your choice) are Easy. You may change your Heartseeker by spending a Downtime Action training with a new weapon.
  * **Animal Companion:** A loyal wild beast companion with 1 Heart of health and three +1 Stats (including Armor) of your choice. It acts directly after your turn in the round order. Once per round, you can command it to move and make an Attempt as a Free Action; on a success, it deals Weapon Effort.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Dire Training Whistle:** Through intense wilderness training, your companion permanently scales in size and ferocity. It becomes a 2 Heart beast, and gains an additional +1 to all of its Stats.
  * **Quiver of the Wild Wind:** A leather quiver blessed by forest spirits. Once per turn, when you make a successful ranged weapon attack, you can choose to let your arrows split mid-flight. Roll your Weapon Effort and distribute the damage freely among up to three Near targets.
  * **Nature's Rune-Stone:** A smooth river stone or other keepsake. It contains the memory of one Druid Spell of your choice that can be cast as a Ritual using your WIS stat.
  * **Amulet of the Swift Hunt:** A necklace of hawk feathers and runic stones. When you take a Move on your turn, both you and your Animal Companion can leap across gaps and cover up to Far distance in a single motion without taking up your Action slot.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Coordinated Pincer:** Once per Room, you can execute a flawless tactical pincer with your beast. Choose a target. Both you and your Animal Companion immediately make a simultaneous attack Attempt against them. If both attacks succeed, the target is completely pinned in place, and your combined strike deals Triple Ultimate Effort (3d12).

### DRUID
Primal conduits of Urth who are strictly forbidden from carrying or wearing refined metal. Their magic is not studied from paper or books, but painfully inked directly into their skin as permanent, living tattoos.

* **ROLE:** Primal Metamorphosis, Permanent Spellcasting, and Natural Defense
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 WIS:** Crucial for casting your elemental tattoos, sensing primal rifts, and tracking wild beasts.
  * **+1 CON:** Essential for enduring the raw physical strain of Wild Shape and maintaining focus.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Tattoos (2):** You begin your career with 2 Druid Spells of your choice inked as tattoos on your body. These tattoos do not need to be equipped, but they permanently occupy 2 of your 20 inventory slots and cannot be unequipped; they can only be replaced with a new tattoo using a Downtime Action.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Totem of the Great Chimera:** Your physical morphing abilities scale drastically. Your Wild Shape can now transform you into a Medium, 2 Heart Animal with four +1 Stats that has one additional Tag (e.g. Swimming, Flying, Echo-Location, etc.) and whose claws/jaws do Weapon Effort.
  * **Bramble-Spike Mantle:** A mantle of thick, living briar thorns that wrap around your shoulders. Whenever an enemy hits you with a Close melee attack, the thorns automatically lash back, dealing d4 Basic Effort (or d6 Weapon Effort if you are in beast form) to the attacker.
  * **Needle of the World-Tree:** A pure bone needle used to channel pain into power. Once per session, you can cast one of your known tattoo spells as a Free Action without making an Attempt roll. To do this, you must take 2 HP of unreducible damage.
  * **Seed of the World-Tree:** A massive, golden acorn that hums with ancient life. Once per day, during a Downtime Action or when not in combat time, you can plant this seed to cast a massive, immediate Healing Nova Ritual. This restores 2 Hearts of HP (20 HP) distributed freely among any allies within Near range.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Wrath of the Wilds:** Once per Room, you can let loose a primal roar and awaken the landscape around you for 1d4 rounds. While this mastery is activated, the ground within Far range of you erupts with thrashing vines; enemies must succeed on a DEX check or be immediately immobilized. Additionally, all of your successful spellcasting Attempts and Wild Shape attacks deal Ultimate Effort (d12).

### SORCERER
A volatile, high-stakes caster whose body serves as a direct conduit for metaphysical forces. Sorcerers do not use spellbooks; they contain spellcraft directly in their bloodstream. While their spells do not count against standard equipped limits, they occupy carried inventory slots to represent the intense physical strain of containing raw magic.

* **ROLE:** Innate Arcane Siphoning, Spellburn, and High-Energy Explosions
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 INT:** Used for your innate spellcasting Attempts, drawing raw power directly from the Ley-Lines.
  * **+1 CON:** Vital for surviving the violent physical back-voltage of spellburn and explosive backfires.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **3 Active Bloodline Spells:** You begin your career with 3 Sorcerer Spells of your choice active in your bloodline. These spells do not count against your Equipped limits, but permanently occupy 3 carried inventory slots. You can only add, remove, or replace spells in your inventory during a Downtime Action.
  * **Heartshard:** Before making a spell Attempt, you may channel your lifeforce through the Heartshard to empower the spell. Take 1d4 damage and choose one: +1d6 Effort, increase range by one step, one additional target, or the Attempt becomes Easy.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Shatterstar Battery:** An unstable crystal device. Whenever you roll a natural 1 on a Spell Attempt, the battery absorbs the feedback instead of you, becoming Charged. On your next successful spellcast, you discharge the battery, adding Ultimate Effort (d12) to your Magic Effort.
  * **Heart of the Phoenix:** A burning ruby charm. When your Hit Points reach 0 or below, you can trigger a fiery explosion. All Close enemies take d8 Magic Effort as fire damage. You regain 1 Heart of HP, but you must permanently reduce your INT by 1 in place of the impacted Stat.
  * **Amulet of the Ley-Line:** A gold necklace. Once per Room, when you successfully cast a single-target spell, you can duplicate the spell to target a second Near enemy. You must make a separate casting Attempt for the second target, but the Attempt is Easy.
  * **Locus of the Untamed Wild:** You gain a permanent +1 to all Magic Effort rolls. Additionally, your raw magical output is so instinctual that you can cast spells even while physically bound, grappled, or pinned without making your Attempts Hard.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Metamagic Overload:** Once per Room, you can throw open the floodgates of your innate power for 1d4 rounds. While overloaded, you can attempt to cast two INT spells on your turn instead of one (the second Attempt is treated as Hard), and any time you roll Magic Effort, your damage dice explode on a result of 7 or 8.

### WARLOCK
A transaction-based caster who bargains with dark, extraplanar patrons. Warlocks do not study magic; they host it. Each spell is a carried inventory item, and their total capacity is limited by their personal Charisma. Changing their spell list requires a tense downtime negotiation roll against their Patron's fluctuating target.

* **ROLE:** At-Will Eldritch Blasting, Debilitating Curses, and High-Stakes Bargaining
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 CHA:** Essential for spell attempts, Eldritch Blasts, and directly defining your maximum spell carrying capacity.
  * **+1 CON:** To survive the physical back-voltage of channeling alien, multi-dimensional planes of energy.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Eldritch Blast:** Fired as an Action up to Far range, dealing Magic Effort on a successful CHA Attempt. You may use an Eldritch Blast to dual wield and it does not occupy an inventory slot.
  * **Pact Spell:** You begin your career with 1 Warlock Spell of your choice occupying an inventory slot.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Brand of the Hexer:** A runic brand burned into your palm. As a Simple Action, you can place an active Hex on a visible enemy. While Hexed, any time that enemy takes damage from your spells or Eldritch Blast, they take additional Basic Effort as necrotic damage. If a Hexed target is slain, you immediately siphon their life force and heal 3 HP.
  * **Tome of Lost Whispers:** +1 CHA. A soot-covered grimoire. When you use a Downtime Action to bargain against your Patron's Target to add, remove, or swap spells, your CHA Attempt is always Easy.
  * **Mantle of the Abyssal Void:** A tattered black cloak. When you are targeted by a physical or magical attack, you can use a Simple Action to phase out, gaining +3 Armor against that incoming attack. If the attack misses you, you can instantly teleport to any visible location within Near range.
  * **Reaper's Sacrificial Athame:** A curved dagger. Whenever you score a killing blow on an enemy using a weapon or your Eldritch Blast, your patron is highly pleased. Your very next spell cast or Eldritch Blast automatically deals Ultimate Effort (d12).
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Eldritch Frenzy:** Once per Room, you can yield fully to your patron's influence, entering a terrifying state of dark alignment for 1d4 rounds. While in this frenzy: you can fire your Eldritch Blast twice per turn as a standard Action (the second is a Hard Attempt), and any time you roll Magic Effort, your damage dice explode on a 7 or an 8.

### CLERIC
Armored crusaders who serve as direct conduits of divine favor. Rather than slotting individual spells, Clerics carry a single equipped Deity's Prayer Book that grants instant access to all prayers of their deity's associated Types, leaving their inventory free for protective steel and holy weapons.

* **ROLE:** Divine Healing, Area Protection, and Holy Smite Magic
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 WIS:** Used for all of your holy Prayer casting Attempts and sensing supernatural corruption in the Glimmer-Gloom.
  * **+1 STR:** For delivering heavy melee smites and carrying holy weapons.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Deity's Prayer Book:** Holds all of your deity's sacred prayers from one of their Domains. As long as you have your Deity's Prayer Book actively equipped in 1 slot, you can cast any Holy Prayer belonging to the associated Domain.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Acolyte's Rosary:** A loop of heavy hematite beads. When you use a Downtime Action to transcribe a new holy scroll or prayer matching your deity's Types into your Prayer Book, your WIS Attempt is always Easy and takes half the time. Additionally, all of your healing prayers deal a permanent +1 Magic Effort.
  * **Mace of the Dawnbringer:** A flanged head shaped like a blooming sun. Your weapon attacks with this mace deal Magic Effort (d8) instead of standard Weapon Effort (d6). On a natural attack roll of 15 or higher, the head flashes with blinding light, making the target's next attack attempt Hard.
  * **Stole of the Archon:** A beautiful, gold-embroidered sash worn over your armor. Your blessings are exceptionally long-lasting. When casting any defensive or buffing prayer (such as Prayer or Sanctuary), its duration is doubled.
  * **Amulet of the Sanctum:** A heavy platinum emblem of your order. Under your campaign's custom stat-decay rules, if you are reduced to 0 HP or below, you may immediately make a WIS check against the current Room Target. If successful, your lifeforce anchors: You regain 1 Heart of health, and you do not suffer the -1 Stat reduction.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Holy Aura of the Chosen:** Once per Room, you can lift your holy symbol and become a shining beacon of your deity's direct presence for 1d4 rounds. While this aura is active: all allies within Near range of you receive a +1 bonus to all of their Attempts and Checks and heal 2 HP at the start of each of their turns; additionally, any Undead or evil creatures who enter or start their turn within Near range of you take Magic Effort as Radiant damage and must make a Hard WIS Check or become Frightened of you.

### PALADIN
Heavy frontline crusaders of absolute conviction. Paladins channel their martial zeal into a specialized, single equipped Holy Libram holding up to 3 prayers of their deity's domain. They are famous for their Primal Smite, which infuses their strikes with raw holy wrath to instantly upgrade their Effort into Ultimate.

* **ROLE:** Frontline Defense, Righteous Smites, and Lifeforce Restoration
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 STR:** Essential for delivering crushing melee strikes and holding heavy weaponry.
  * **+1 Armor:** Heavy plate steel training designed to ignore physical complaints.
* **STARTER GEAR/ABILITIES:**
  * **Deity's Holy Libram:** Its thick parchment pages are reinforced with fine duranium wiring, and the cover is deeply engraved with the sacred glyph of your chosen deity. It holds up to three of your deity's prayers and allows you to use them while you have the Libram equipped. Equipping the Libram requires one hand.
  * **Primal Smite:** Your weapon strikes are infused with holy wrath. Choose an enemy type for the focus of your wrath (unholy, demonic, undead); when you roll Effort (Basic, Weapon, or Magic) for a melee attack against an enemy of that type, the Attempt's Effort is automatically upgraded to Ultimate.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Crucible of Retribution:** A glowing iron clasp worn on your weapon arm. When you use your reaction to absorb an attack for an ally, or when you take damage from an enemy, you store that kinetic energy. On your next turn, you can channel this pain into your next successful attack, adding the exact amount of damage you just took directly to your Weapon Effort roll.
  * **Sun-Tempered Breastplate:** A magnificent breastplate of polished brass and silver. This armor grants you +2 Armor. Additionally, you are completely immune to any damage or negative status effects caused by fire or radiant energy, and your armor sheds bright light in a Close radius.
  * **Tome of the High Paragon:** A gold-leaf insert. This holy upgrade permanently expands your equipped Libram. Your Libram can now hold up to 5 spells instead of 3, and you gain access to a second Type of your deity's prayers without violating your singular Libram limit.
  * **Saintly Whetstone:** A block of pure white marble blessed by a high archon. Choose one melee weapon. Whenever you roll a maximum result on its Weapon Effort die, the strike erupts with solar fire; roll that die again and add it to the total (exploding damage). Furthermore, this weapon's attacks are treated as Magical and Silver.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Aura of Righteousness:** Once per Room, you can slam your weapon into the ground, let loose a battle cry, and project a 15-foot holy aura for 1d4 rounds. While this aura is active, choose two: all allies within Near range of you receive a +1 Armor bonus and are completely immune to fear, stun, or confusion; your successful melee attacks deal Ultimate Effort (d12); your Lay on Hands pool is temporarily limitless, allowing you to use it on your turn as a Simple Action to heal allies for d6 Basic Effort.

### ROGUE
High-stakes opportunists who rely on perfect timing, swift reflexes, and sheer luck. Rogues bypass standard direct confrontations to target vital pressure points, dealing massive x3 Sneak Attacks with Light weapons and utilizing their legendary Rogue's Luck to instantly bypass otherwise insurmountable obstacles.

* **ROLE:** Light-Weapon Stealth Attacks, Lockpicking, and Luck-Based Bypasses
* **RECOMMENDED STATS:**
  * **+2 DEX:** Vital for sneaking, ranged precision, lockpicking, and reflex evasion.
  * **+1 WIS:** For spotting hidden traps and scouting danger ahead of the team.
* **STARTER GEAR:**
  * **Assassin's Blade:** Any Light weapon gains the ability to do 3x your Weapon Effort if you attack with it while undetected by the target.
* **MILESTONE REWARDS:**
  * **Sorin's Keepsake:** While carrying the Keepsake, you may reroll one failed Stealth Attempt or Check once per Room. You must keep the second result.
  * **Thieves' Gimbel:** A rotating, gold-plated mechanism. All lockpicking and trap disabling Attempts are Easy, and both do Ultimate Effort.
  * **Cloak of the Chimera-Mist:** A charcoal-grey mantle that allows you to slip away when cornered. Under your campaign's custom stat-decay rules: Once per day, when you are reduced to 0 HP or below and trigger your stat-decay recovery, you can instantly throw down a smoke cloud and teleport to any unseen location within Near range. When you do this, you do not suffer the -1 Stat reduction.
  * **Smoke Censer:** Once per Room, as a Simple Action, create a cloud of smoke filling a Near area within Near range. Checks and Attempts inside the smoke are Hard, except Stealth and Evasion Attempts which are Easy. The smoke disperses after 1d4 rounds.
* **MASTERY REWARD:**
  * **Rogue's Luck:** Once per Room, after you land a successful Attempt of any kind, you can throw caution to the wind and let luck decide the outcome. Force the target of your Attempt to make a check against the Room Target.
    * **If Living:** The target must make a CON check.
    * **If Inanimate (e.g., locks, doors, complex machinery):** The target must make a Heart check (using its current Heart/Effort rating as its roll modifier).
    * **On Failure:** The target is instantly and automatically overcome.
    * **On Success:** The target resists your luck, but your strike or check still lands normally, dealing Effort toward overcoming it.

### BARBARIAN
The brute-force specialists of the high fells and Glimmer-Gloom, the Barbarian enters a state of primal fury to tear through duranium vault doors and heavy armor with massive, unrefined weaponry.

## HOUSE RULES

### ACTIONS
On your turn you may move up to Near distances along with a Basic Action (e.g. attack, cast a spell), or you may forgo your Action and move Far. Other Actions are defined below.

* **Simple:** A Simple Action is a quick, minor action performed during your turn. You may take one Simple Action per turn in addition to your normal Action. Simple Actions include quick interactions, drawing or stowing an item, opening an unlocked door, and using abilities that specifically require a Simple Action. A Simple Action should not replace a full Action or require a significant Attempt unless the ability says otherwise.
* **Reaction:** A Reaction is an action taken outside your normal turn in response to a specific trigger. You can take one Reaction per round unless an ability grants an additional Reaction. Resolve the Reaction immediately when its trigger occurs, following the ability's timing.
* **Free:** A Free Action is an action that requires essentially no time or effort and does not consume your Action, Simple Action, or Reaction. You may take Free Actions whenever their effects or the fiction allow, but the GM may limit repeated or unreasonable Free Actions.

### PROGRESSIVE ATTEMPTS
Some tasks require sustained effort over multiple Attempts rather than a single Success or Failure. These are called Progressive Attempts. When making a Progressive Attempt, roll the appropriate Attempt and Effort as normal. The outcome determines how much Effort is applied toward completing the task:

* **Critical Success:** On a Natural 20, regardless of the Target or any modifiers you double your rolled Effort, or are provided another, suitable boon determined by the GM.
* **Success:** Apply the full Effort rolled.
* **Success, But...:** Apply half the Effort rolled (round down, minimum 1), or suffer another appropriate complication determined by the GM.
* **Critical Failure:** On a Natural 1, regardless of the Target or any modifiers, no progress is made and an appropriate complication occurs at the GM's discretion.

Effort accumulates between Attempts until the task's required Effort is reached. Multiple characters can contribute Effort toward the same task. Each Attempt consumes the normal time, action, and resources required to perform it.

### DISTANCES
* **Beyond Far:** Anything farther than Far, at GM discretion. Beyond Far is typically limited to out of combat spells.

### KARMA SYSTEM
* All players start a session with 3 Karma.
* A player may spend any amount of Karma to add +1 to a roll.
* A player may spend all of their Karma (if they have more than 1) to make any roll a 20.
* When a player rolls a 1 on any Attempt or Check, they increment their Karma by 1 (max 6).

### DEATH AND DYING
When a character is reduced to 0 Hit Points or below, instead of falling unconscious and letting a D6 decide your fate, you immediately reduce a pertinent Stat by 1 and become Weakened.

The stat you lose should match whatever just walloped you:
* Beaten to a pulp by an ogre? Kiss a point of STR or CON goodbye.
* Mind-blasted by a cosmic horror? Say farewell to a point of INT or WIS.

Death and Dying resolves immediately when a character reaches 0 HP or below, unless an ability explicitly triggers before Death and Dying resolves. You cannot reduce any stat below 0. If all the stats relevant to your current beating are already sitting at 0, your character has officially run out of things to sacrifice. At this point, the fight is over for you and you are Dead.

### RECOVERING STATS
These stat reductions are semi-permanent. You can't just take a quick "Moment" or an "Hour" nap to feel better; your bones are still rattling. These penalties can only be healed outside of the game session - during Downtime, occupying dedicated "Days" of absolute safety.

### DOWNTIME
Each session includes two Downtime periods: Opening Downtime and Closing Downtime. During each period, every character receives two Downtime Actions, for a total of four per session. These Actions allow Agents to maintain their Credent status, manage their finances, acquire equipment, repair Relics, recover lost Stats, and pursue opportunities beyond their official Agency duties.

Available Actions include Stat Restoration, Asset Liquidation, Freelance Consultation, Asset Acquisition, Capital Investment, Maintenance, Identify a Relic, and Research. Most Actions involve an Attempt against the City Target or a relevant Faction Target. A Clean Success accomplishes the intended task under normal conditions, while a Success, But... accomplishes the task at the cost of an additional complication, obligation, or unwanted attention. Some Actions, such as Capital Investment, require no roll. Downtime is also an opportunity to establish lasting contacts, investigate personal interests, and make arrangements that may influence future adventures.

Downtime is not necessarily safe or uneventful. The GM can use the Agents' activities to advance existing threats, introduce complications, reveal secrets, bring NPCs and factions into play, present opportunities, threaten things the Agents value, provide unexpected rewards, or fundamentally transform an ongoing situation. The Agents may be between missions, but the world continues to move around them.

## UNIVERSAL SPELLCASTING RULES

* **No Prepared Spells:** Spellcasters do not prepare spells at the start of each day. Spells are governed strictly by physical carrying limits, active inventory slot requirements, or permanent physical markings.
* **Casting Stats:**
  * **Wizards and Sorcerers** cast using Intelligence (INT).
  * **Clerics, Paladins, Rangers, and Druids** cast using Wisdom (WIS).
  * **Warlocks and Bards** cast using Charisma (CHA).
* **Rituals:** Spells that are cast strictly during Downtime Actions or on Turns when the game is not in Continuous (Combat) Time, unless otherwise specified.
* **Uncommon:** All spells in the Spell List are Uncommon for the purpose of acquisition or disposition.
* **Duplicates:** If a spell appears on multiple lists, it is the same spell unless otherwise stated.
* **Reaction:** A spell may be cast as a Reaction only when its description explicitly says "Reaction."
* **Consumable:** Spells found in the wild or acquired as physical items (such as spell scrolls created by Wizards) are single-use items. Once cast (regardless of Success or Failure), the spell is used up and is removed from the character's inventory.

## CLASS-SPECIFIC MAGIC & SPELLBOOK RULES

### WIZARD
* **Equipped Spells:** You must physically carry and have actively equipped each spell you want to cast in a spellbook. Each active spell occupies 1 Equipped inventory slot.
* **Transcribing:** Spells found or bought during adventures must be copied into your spellbook during a Downtime Action through Research & Development by making a successful INT Attempt against the Room Target.
* **Starting Spells:** You begin with 2 Wizard Spells of your choice already copied into starting spellbooks.

### SORCERER
* **Bloodline Slots:** Your spells do not count against your 10 Equipped gear limits, but each spell you know permanently occupies 1 carried inventory slot, representing the physical strain of hosting raw magic.
* **Downtime Tuning:** Spells can only be added, removed, or replaced in your inventory during a Downtime Action through Research & Development with a CON Attempt against the Room Target.
* **Starting Spells:** You begin with 3 Sorcerer Spells of your choice already active in your bloodline.

### WARLOCK
* **Charisma Cap:** Your spells do not count against Equipped gear limits, but each known spell occupies 1 carried inventory slot. The total number of spells you can actively carry is strictly limited to your Charisma (CHA) stat (base + loot/milestone bonuses).
* **The Patron's Target:** Adding, removing, or changing your carried spells during a Downtime Action through Research & Development and requires a successful CHA Attempt against your Patron's Target.
* **Patron Target DC:** This target difficulty starts at 12 and permanently moves up or down based on your standing, alignment, and completed favors with your patron.

### CLERIC
* **The Single-Book Rule:** You do not need to occupy separate inventory slots for individual spells. As long as you have one of your Deity's Prayer Books actively equipped in 1 Equipped slot, you have instant access to all of your deity's available prayers from the book's Domain.
* **Divine Types:** Cleric and Paladin spells are divided into specific Domains (e.g., Life, Protection, Retribution), and each deity is associated with one or more of these Domains.

### PALADIN
* **Libram Requirement:** As a divine half-caster, you cannot cast spells unless you have a physical Deity's Libram actively equipped.
* **Possession Limit:** You may only have 1 Libram in your possession (equipped or in your carried inventory) at any time.
* **Capacity & Downtime:** The Libram is a Starter Reward option that holds a maximum of 3 spells of your deity's associated Type. Swapping or changing these spells out requires a Downtime Action involving Research & Development.

### BARD
* **Songwriting:** Bards do not find or buy pre-written spells. Instead, during a Research & Development Downtime Action, you can take any spell from any spell list, roll a CHA Attempt against the City Target, and on a success record it as a new, equipped Spellsong.
* **Evocative Naming:** Every Spellsong you compose must be written down with an evocative, poetic name that reflects its magic (e.g., *"Cressida's Shimmering Lullaby"*).
* **Instrument Constraint:** Spellsongs count against standard equipment limits, and each song occupies 1 Equipped inventory slot. To cast a Spellsong, you must actively play your equipped musical instrument (occupying at least one hand).

### DRUID
* **Living Tattoos:** Your magic is permanently inked directly into your flesh. Druid spells do not need to be equipped, but each known spell permanently occupies 1 of your 20 inventory slots, representing the physical and spiritual toll of elementally binding with Urth.
* **Unerasable Ink:** Once a tattoo is added to your body, it cannot be unequipped or discarded. It can only be replaced with a completely new spell by spending a Research & Development Downtime Action to chemically scar, burn, or rewrite the tattoo.
* **Auxiliary Health Buffer:** While transformed, you gain a separate 1 Heart (10 HP) health pool. Any damage you take is subtracted from this beast pool first. If this pool is reduced to 0 HP, you immediately revert to your normal form, and any leftover damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
* **Beast Stats:** You gain three +1 Stats of your choice to add to your rolls, matching the physical nature of the chosen animal (e.g., +1 DEX, +1 CON, and +1 WIS for a quick forest wolf).
* **Natural Attacks:** You cannot wield manufactured weapons or cast other spells while transformed. Instead, your claws, jaws, or talons deal standard Weapon Effort (d6) damage.
* **Form Tag:** Your beast form grants you one locomotive or sensory Tag (such as climbing, swimming, flying, or darkvision).

### RANGER
* **Ritual Casting Only:** You are restricted to casting Druid spells, but you cannot channel them instantly in the heat of battle. You may only cast them as Rituals.
* **Memories as Spells:** Your spells are memories that you associate with items. During a Downtime Action, spend a Research & Development action reflecting in order to associate a memory with an object. That object becomes the Item for your Ritual.

## CONDITIONS

Conditions are fictional first. A Condition describes what is happening in the fiction; its mechanical effect is the minimum rule necessary to represent it. If a specific ability gives a Condition a different duration or removal method, that ability overrides the general rule.

* **Blinded:** You cannot see. Any Attempt or Check requiring sight is Hard or impossible, as appropriate. Attacks against unseen targets are Hard unless the attacker has another reliable way to locate them.
* **Burning:** A Burning creature takes d4 Basic Effort at the start of its turn. At the end of its turn, it may make a CON Check vs. the Room Target to extinguish the flames. A creature may instead spend its entire turn extinguishing itself or another creature, automatically ending the Burning condition.
* **Charmed:** Treats the source of the Charm as it would a close, personal friend.
* **Confused:** Your Attempts and Checks requiring deliberate judgment are Hard.
* **Frightened:** You cannot willingly move closer to the source of your fear. Attempts or Checks made directly against the source are Hard. At the end of your turn, you may make a WIS Check vs. the Room Target to end the condition.
* **Grappled:** You cannot move away from the creature or object grappling you. You may still attack, cast, or take other actions normally. On your turn, you may make a STR or DEX Check vs. the grappler's STR/DEX to break free. A successful check ends the Grappled condition.
* **Immobilized:** You cannot move from your current location. You may still attack, cast spells, defend yourself, and take other actions normally. At the end of your turn, you may make an appropriate STR, DEX, or CON Check vs. Room Target to end the condition, if the source of the effect allows it.
* **Prone:** You are knocked to the ground. Standing up requires your Move, and your next Action is Hard. While Prone, ranged attacks against you are Hard and melee attacks against you are Easy.
* **Slowed:** Movement is limited to Close.
* **Stunned:** You cannot take Actions, Reactions, or make Attempts. You may still defend yourself normally. The condition ends at the end of your next turn unless the effect specifies otherwise.
* **Weakened:** Imposed when any Stat has been reduced due to Death & Dying.

* **Combat Casting:** WIS Attempt vs. Room Target. You call down searing holy ash on a target within Near range. The target must succeed on a CON Check vs. the Room Target or go blind for 1d4 rounds. While blind, all of the target's Checks and Attempts are Hard.
* **Out-of-Combat Casting:** Not applicable (combat-only negative status).

#### COMMAND
* **Combat Casting:** Speak a single, booming word to a target within Near range (e.g., "Yield," "Flee," "Drop"), forcing them to make a WIS Check vs. the Room Target. On a failure, the target must spend its next Action performing the command if it can reasonably do so. The command cannot directly cause the target to harm itself.
* **Out-of-Combat Casting:** Not applicable.

#### COMMUNE
* **Out-of-Combat Casting:** Automatic Success. Spend 10 minutes in quiet, uninterrupted prayer to ask your deity a single question that can be answered with a "yes" or "no". Attempting this more than once per day or session angers the cosmic powers, resulting in an automatic Holy Backfire.

* **Out-of-Combat Casting:** Automatic Success. Spending 10 minutes in holy meditation manifests enough pure water and nourishing bread to sustain 3 Medium creatures for 1 day.

* **Combat Casting:** WIS Attempt vs. Room Target. Touch 1 creature to completely purge all active magical and mundane diseases from their body.

* **Combat Casting:** WIS Attempt vs. Room Target. Touch 1 creature to completely neutralize and flush all poisons from their bloodstream.
* **Out-of-Combat Casting:** Automatic Success. Touch a poisoned creature outside of combat to instantly cleanse them.

#### CURE WOUNDS
* **Combat Casting:** WIS Attempt vs. Room Target. Cure Wounds may be used as a Reaction when an ally reaches 0 HP, before Death and Dying resolves. On success, the target regains 1 Heart and does not suffer the Stat reduction from Death and Dying. A creature can benefit from Cure Wounds only once per Room.
